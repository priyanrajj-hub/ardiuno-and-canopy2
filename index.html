Hardware & Sensor Mapping

This matrix serves as the unified hardware ground-truth for the system. Note: Certain sensors (like the Rain and IR sensors) may exhibit floating values when partially unplugged indoors.

DHT11 (Temp/Humidity)
Tied to Arduino D3. Tracks ambient vegetative climate. Generates AL_TEMPH / AL_TEMPL alerts.
MQ Gas Sensor
Analog AO -> ADS1115 A2 | Digital DO -> Arduino D4. Highly sensitive to indoor organic compounds.
Capacitive Soil Sensor
Analog AO -> ADS1115 A0. Measures dielectric permittivity of surrounding medium.
pH Interface Board
Analog PO -> ADS1115 A3. BNC probe measuring acidic/alkaline biomass levels.
Rain & LDR Sensors
Rain AO -> Arduino A3. LDR AO -> ADS1115 A1. Indoor testing usually saturates rain values due to module variance.
IR & Sound Events
IR OUT -> D5 (Motion detection). Sound -> D13 (Acoustic disturbances).
